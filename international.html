Trading from somewhere else?

REGIONCanada →For United States users →Other countries

Our guides currently cover Canada and the United States. The principles carry over to most countries: keep every receipt, separate business costs from personal ones, and record which currency and exchange rate you used. For the rules where you live, start with your national tax authority.

Pivotext guides are general information, not tax, accounting or legal advice. Confirm anything you rely on with a qualified tax professional in your country.
