Why this exists

Trading is the easy part to track. Everything around it isn't.

01

$150 here. A $99 reset there.

Eval fees go on a personal card at 11pm and never reach the books. By December you've spent thousands you can't account for.

02

Three firms, three dashboards.

Payouts land on different schedules through different processors. Nothing adds them up, so you're copying numbers between tabs.

03

Then April arrives.

Your accountant gets a folder of screenshots and bills you by the hour to make sense of it. Half the deductions never get claimed.

Inside Pivotext

Three screens. That's the whole product.

No trade journal, no analytics dashboard, no advice. It records what you spent and what you were paid, and it hands that to your accountant.

Month end

One button. One document.

Income with the exchange rate used on every payout. Costs split by account and by category. A short list of items your accountant needs to rule on, flagged rather than decided. Plus a CSV they can import straight into Xero or QuickBooks.

Pivotext doesn't classify anything or tell you what's deductible. It gets the numbers in front of the person qualified to decide.
